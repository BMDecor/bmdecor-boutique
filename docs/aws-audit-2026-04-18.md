### B.2 — CloudFront `E1J7AJ5Q3FRB0P` → serves `mta-sts.bmdecor.es`

- **Purpose:** MTA-STS ([RFC 8461](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc8461)) — enforces TLS for email destined to `@bmdecor.es`. Requires a HTTPS endpoint returning a policy file.
- **Origin:** `mta-sts.bmdecor.es.s3.eu-west-3.amazonaws.com` (Paris bucket).
- **Active?** Probably yes — without it, mail servers sending email to `@bmdecor.es` can't verify the MTA-STS policy, which may drop mail. Paired with the DKIM CNAMEs in Route 53.
- **Risk of deletion:** email deliverability could suffer.
- **Proposed action:** leave until we know the client's email plan. If they're using a hosted email service (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365), MTA-STS may be handled there; if Jason self-hosts mail at `mail.ukraine.com.ua` (seen in DNS), then this mta-sts distribution probably matters.

**Approve deletion now?** ☐ **no** (need the client's email setup confirmed first)
